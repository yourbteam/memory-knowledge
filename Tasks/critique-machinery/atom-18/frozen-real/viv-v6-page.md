# Tactical Roadmap

The phased twelve-month sequence of the approved platform: signature moments balanced against an always-on presence, every tactic an Activation Card placed by date, channel, audience, message, phase and KPI (key performance indicator).

**The platform is approved: So You Can, decided by Maria Bengtsson.**
**The owners and the calendar below are not yet approved; no owner is recorded.**

**Period and markets.** The campaign period is a 12-month Vivacom Bulgaria roadmap from Month 1 proof lock to Month 12 recalibration, with local-market adaptation available only where United Group deploys the So You Can toolkit.

## The phases

| phase | objective | months |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Seed | Lock the So You Can proof, claim boundaries, service response lines and internal readiness before public launch activity. | 1 · 2 |
| Launch | Introduce So You Can through The Invisible Thread with an earned-led household story and owned proof behind it. | 3 · 4 |

| Amplify | Extend the proof from homes to workplaces, municipalities, public services and credible third parties once evidence is cleared. | 10 · 11 · 12 |

The four phases are the year. Inside a phase, each major moment runs Tease → Launch → Sustain → Amplify; across the year the corporate rollout widens the audience Engage → Enhance → Advocate → Amplify.

## The Hero / Hub / Hygiene balance

**Hero — signature moments, about a fifth.** Hero effort is concentrated in The Invisible Thread at Home, Beyond the Home Case Stories and the Credible Third-Party Visibility Push, giving the year a small set of signature So You Can moments.

**Hub — sustained programmes, about three fifths.** Hub effort carries most of the work through internal alignment, launch reinforcement, the Household Proof Series, Reach Clearly Explained and the Advocacy Proof Kit.

**Hygiene — always-on maintenance, about a fifth.** Hygiene effort protects the platform through proof lock, service response lines, the Conversation Management Loop, The Observatory Newsroom and the year-close scorecard.

## The Activation Cards

No tactic goes on the calendar until it is a card.

### Proof and Claim Lock

**Insight.** The So You Can platform fails if reach, service or household proof outruns the evidence customers and stakeholders can check.

**Idea.** Lock the approved proof, do-not-say lines and The Invisible Thread claim boundaries before anything public runs.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Use internal working sessions and written clearance to align Legal and Regulatory Affairs, leaders and communications teams before launch.

**Desired outcome.** Vivacom enters launch with one cleared proof base for So You Can and no broad network or size claim in market.  ·  **Targets.** By the end of Month 1, the claim register is read for every household, service and reach claim, with source, clearance status and do-not-say risk visible.  ·  **Approver.** Elitza Georgieva

**Signature.** So You Can / The Invisible Thread proof lock  ·  **KPI.** Priority claims with named proof source — percentage of claims in the register, So You Can claim register · Do-not-say risk resolved before release — integer count of unresolved risks, pre-publish governance checklist  ·  **Month.** 1

### Service Moment Response Lines

**Insight.** A service issue can prove or break The Invisible Thread in public.

**Idea.** Build plain-language updates for outages, bills, app payments, contract questions and help moments; live, unpromoted, until the Launch moment in Month 3.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Publish first through owned service channels when needed, then support through shared updates and earned response lines if the issue becomes public.

**Desired outcome.** Customers see service friction answered as part of the So You Can promise, not treated as a side issue.  ·  **Targets.** From Month 1 through Month 12, the service response register is read weekly and after each visible service issue for approval speed, clarity and repeated question themes.  ·  **Approver.** Elitza Georgieva

**Signature.** The Invisible Thread in service  ·  **KPI.** Approved service response time — median hours from issue log to approved line, service response register · Customer understanding of service updates — 1-to-5 clarity score, plain-language service update rubric  ·  **Months.** 1 to 12

### Conversation Management Loop

**Insight.** The platform cannot hold reputation between bursts if nobody is listening, triaging, responding and escalating every day.

**Idea.** Run the daily listen-triage-respond-escalate loop under So You Can so service questions and proof opportunities are caught before they drift.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Read social, service, app-review and earned-media signals daily, route them to the right owner and feed cleared language back into response teams.

**Desired outcome.** Vivacom keeps The Invisible Thread credible between campaign moments.  ·  **Targets.** Daily from Month 1 through Month 12, the conversation log is read for live issue themes, escalation status, approved response use and advocacy opportunities.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** So You Can response loop  ·  **KPI.** Escalated issues with owner and next action recorded — percentage of escalated issues, conversation management log · Recurring customer questions converted into approved answers — integer count of question themes closed, question-theme register  ·  **Months.** 1 to 12

### The Observatory Newsroom

**Insight.** Moments worth entering close quickly, and The Invisible Thread needs cleared proof before Vivacom speaks into them.

**Idea.** Run a newsroom watch that spots relevant household, service, reach and stakeholder moments for So You Can; live, unpromoted, until the Launch moment in Month 3.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Read media, competitor, regulator, service and community signals, then prepare cleared owned, earned and shared lines for moments worth entering.

**Desired outcome.** Vivacom enters relevant moments with proof-led lines instead of late or broad claims.  ·  **Targets.** Weekly from Month 2 through Month 12, the newsroom watchlist is read for moment fit, proof readiness, channel route and whether the moment should be entered or left quiet.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** The Observatory for So You Can  ·  **KPI.** Relevant moments with cleared entry line before window closes — percentage of entered moments, Observatory watchlist · Moments rejected for weak proof before publishing — integer count of rejected moments, moment-fit decision log  ·  **Months.** 2 to 12

### Internal Alignment and Spokesperson Pack

**Insight.** Spokespeople can only protect the So You Can idea if they know the proof, the guardrails and the words not to use.

**Idea.** Brief leaders, service teams and spokespeople on The Invisible Thread, the proof base, audience messages and do-not-say lines before public launch; live, unpromoted, until the Launch moment in Month 3.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Use internal briefings, a 30-second pitch, spokesperson materials and Q&A (questions and answers) rehearsal to test whether teams can use approved lines.

**Desired outcome.** The organisation can explain So You Can consistently before the first earned story lands.  ·  **Targets.** By the end of Month 2, the briefing record is read for attendance, approved-language use in rehearsal and unresolved spokesperson questions.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** So You Can spokesperson pack  ·  **KPI.** Briefed teams passing approved-language rehearsal — percentage of participating teams, spokesperson rehearsal rubric · Unresolved launch questions after briefing — integer count of open questions, internal Q&A register  ·  **Month.** 2

### The Invisible Thread at Home

**Insight.** Existing customers judge Vivacom first through ordinary household moments: internet, television, bills, app payments, outages and help.

**Idea.** Launch So You Can through one named household story that shows The Invisible Thread helping daily life continue without asking people to admire the system.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Lead through earned media, then drive readers to owned proof pages and shared assets that explain the service and support behind the story.

**Desired outcome.** Existing customers begin to see Vivacom as useful, warmer and proof-led.  ·  **Targets.** In Month 3, the launch coverage report and owned proof-page analytics are read after the earned launch and again at month close.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** #SoYouCan / The Invisible Thread starts at home  ·  **KPI.** Earned stories carrying the household proof message — percentage of launch coverage, earned launch quality report · Proof-page visits from launch routes — integer count of tagged visits, owned proof-page analytics report  ·  **Month.** 3

### Household Launch Reinforcement

**Insight.** The first household story needs repetition in owned and shared channels before it becomes a usable platform memory.

**Idea.** Use the social pack and owned proof pages to repeat The Invisible Thread at home under So You Can after the earned launch.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Publish approved posts, route paid support to proof pages and answer household questions through the response loop.

**Desired outcome.** The launch idea moves from one story into a recognisable household proof pattern.  ·  **Targets.** In Month 4, the social pack report and proof-page analytics are read weekly for approved post engagement, repeat visits and service-friction themes.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** #SoYouCan at home  ·  **KPI.** Approved household posts with proof-page route used — percentage of published posts, social pack publishing checklist · Repeat visits to household proof pages — integer count of tagged repeat visits, owned proof-page analytics report  ·  **Month.** 4

### Household Proof Series

**Insight.** Vivacom is most believable when it proves the relationship customers already use every day.

**Idea.** Publish a recurring owned hub series connecting fixed internet, television, coverage-experience reach and help as one household relationship under The Invisible Thread.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Use owned stories as the proof home, feed selected stories to influencers and use paid and shared routes to bring readers back to the proof.

**Desired outcome.** The household relationship becomes the repeatable proof of So You Can, not a one-off launch story.  ·  **Targets.** Monthly from Month 5 through Month 12, the series dashboard is read for repeat visits, shared engagement, influencer proof use and affinity-tracker movement when the tracker is available.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** The Invisible Thread at home  ·  **KPI.** Returning readers to household proof stories — percentage of tagged story readers, household proof series dashboard · Influencer or creator references using approved proof — integer count of approved references, influencer proof-use register  ·  **Months.** 5 to 12

### Reach, Clearly Explained

**Insight.** Coverage-experience proof can support reach, but it cannot support claims about speed, reliability or overall quality.

**Idea.** Create claim-cleared explainers and media lines that show how Vivacom proves reach while keeping The Invisible Thread inside approved evidence.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Brief media and stakeholders with the source boundary visible, then amplify the cleared explanation through owned and selective paid channels.

**Desired outcome.** Regulators, media and parent-group stakeholders see a disciplined proof story instead of a broad network claim.  ·  **Targets.** Monthly from Month 6 through Month 12, the reach wording report is read for approved wording pull-through, unsupported network claims and clearance status.  ·  **Approver.** Elitza Georgieva

**Signature.** Reach, clearly explained  ·  **KPI.** Coverage using the approved reach wording — percentage of relevant earned items, reach wording media audit · Unsupported network claims caught before release — integer count of blocked claims, Legal and Regulatory Affairs clearance register  ·  **Months.** 6 to 12

### Expert and Advocacy Proof Kit

**Insight.** Advocacy asked for before people understand the proof becomes noise.

**Idea.** Equip employee advocates, service-team champions, credible alumni and partner advocates trained by the Conversation Management Loop with approved So You Can proof and disclosure guidance.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Give advocates proof cards, draft lines, disclosure rules and escalation routes, then read their use before widening participation.

**Desired outcome.** The people closest to the proof can carry The Invisible Thread without drifting into unsupported claims.  ·  **Targets.** Monthly from Month 8 through Month 11, the advocacy register is read for approved proof use, disclosure status, questions raised and lines needing correction.  ·  **Approver.** Maria Bengtsson

**Signature.** So You Can advocacy kit  ·  **KPI.** Advocate posts or briefings using approved proof without correction — percentage of reviewed advocate outputs, advocacy proof-use register · Disclosure-compliant advocate outputs — percentage of reviewed advocate outputs, creator and advocate disclosure checklist  ·  **Months.** 8 to 11

### Beyond the Home Case Stories

**Insight.** The household proof logic can travel only when workplace, municipal and public-service examples are named, current and cleared.

**Idea.** Publish cleared case stories that show So You Can extending The Invisible Thread from homes to workplaces, municipalities and public services.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Use earned stories, owned case pages, events and stakeholder briefings only after partner and claim clearance.

**Desired outcome.** The platform widens without becoming a broad leadership or scale claim.  ·  **Targets.** Monthly from Month 10 through Month 12, the case clearance tracker is read for named-party approval, claim clearance, stakeholder engagement and qualified interest.  ·  **Approver.** Iva Gajić

### Credible Third-Party Visibility Push

**Insight.** For corporate reputation, third-party proof carries more trust than paid reach alone.

**Idea.** Use professional influencers, analysts, editors and respected partners to show that So You Can is proved through visible relationships, not size claims.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Brief credible third parties with the approved 30-second pitch, proof notes and do-not-say lines, then amplify only compliant earned and shared outputs.

**Desired outcome.** The platform gains visible validation without losing claim discipline.  ·  **Targets.** In Month 11, the third-party visibility report is read for approved-language use, earned quality, disclosure compliance and do-not-say breaches.  ·  **Approver.** Iva Gajić

**Signature.** Proof you can see  ·  **KPI.** Third-party outputs using approved So You Can language — percentage of reviewed outputs, third-party language audit · Do-not-say breaches in amplified third-party material — integer count of breaches, visibility compliance log  ·  **Month.** 11

### Year-Close Scorecard and Recalibration

**Insight.** The platform only improves if reputation, service, proof and channel signals are read together.

**Idea.** Close the So You Can year with one executive scorecard across paid, earned, shared, owned, service and reputation signals.

**Engagement — the mechanic.** Read the year's tagged results, tracker movement, service themes, earned quality and proof performance, then set the next-cycle recalibration.

**Desired outcome.** Vivacom can see what The Invisible Thread proved, what weakened it and what should change next year.  ·  **Targets.** In Month 12, the executive scorecard is read with all tagged PESO (Paid, Earned, Shared, Owned), service and reputation measures available for annual review.  ·  **Approver.** Asen Velikov

## The always-on loop

Underneath the phases runs the Conversation Management loop — supported by the always-on newsroom running proactive stories and reactive issues management: establish goals → gather assets → develop proprietary content → deploy across owned, earned and paid → train advocates → deploy the trained advocates → measure → refine. The advocates are employee advocates, service-team champions, credible alumni and partner advocates, equipped by Expert and Advocacy Proof Kit in Month 8; they deploy from Month 8, and until then the loop runs its other steps without them.

## The proof-building order

**Cases first** — Months 1 to 12: Proof and Claim Lock, Service Moment Response Lines, Conversation Management Loop, Internal Alignment and Spokesperson Pack, The Invisible Thread at Home, Household Launch Reinforcement, Household Proof Series, Beyond the Home Case Stories. This stage proves that So You Can can be shown through cleared household, service and institution cases before Vivacom asks anyone to believe a broader reputation claim.

**Then thought leadership** — Months 2 to 12: The Observatory Newsroom, Reach, Clearly Explained, Expert and Advocacy Proof Kit. This stage proves that The Invisible Thread can enter public conversations with disciplined reach, service and advocacy language inside the approved evidence boundary.

**Then visibility** — Months 11 to 12: Credible Third-Party Visibility Push, Year-Close Scorecard and Recalibration. This stage proves that the platform has earned outside validation and can be measured before the next cycle is widened.

## The rollout

**First audience.** Existing customers, with Maya as the first believer.

**Then widen — from Month 3 to Month 12.** After the Month 3 earned-led household launch, the roadmap widens to the critical urban audience, regulators, media, employees, advocates, business buyers, municipalities, public-sector customers and parent-group stakeholders through cleared proof.
