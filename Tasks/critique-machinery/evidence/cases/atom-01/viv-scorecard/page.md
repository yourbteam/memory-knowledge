## The evaluation narrative

### Objective status — on, at risk or off, and why it happened

The objectives are measurable, but they are not yet ready for launch because several baselines are not established.

### Organizational contribution

The campaign can contribute to preference-led value growth, but it cannot claim to create business growth without a controlled model or matched comparison.

### Outputs, outtakes and outcomes evidence across PESO (Paid, Earned, Shared, Owned)

The framework covers outputs, outtakes, outcomes and impact through earned quality, recall, owned engagement, friction themes, employee understanding and tracker movement.

### Audience and market differences

The first readings must separate existing customers, the critical urban audience, Gen Z, employees and stakeholders because each group starts from a different position.

### What worked, and why

No live result exists yet, but the measurement design works because every metric connects back to So You Can, The Invisible Thread and a named audience change.

### What did not, and why

The pack is not launch-ready until Vivacom opens tracker access, message-recall baseline access, media monitoring, friction data and the employee source.

### Decisions and next actions

The next decision is to hold launch reporting until baselines and targets are set from real readings; the next actions are to confirm owners, sources and dates.

### Limitations and confidence, with three recommendations

Confidence is medium before launch because the logic is clear but the readings are missing. Next time, set the measurement owner before strategy approval, field the message baseline before platform approval and agree any matched comparison before claiming business contribution.

## Decisions and next actions

- **Increase recognition of Vivacom as a warmer, proof-led national role among existing customers.** Change the launch gate by making tracker access and target-setting a pre-launch requirement.
- **Message recall tied to So You Can and The Invisible Thread.** Scale measurement readiness by approving and fielding the recall baseline before launch.
- **Quality earned attention for the platform.** Change the monitoring setup so it records message quality, not volume alone.
- **Priority customer-friction themes.** Change the reporting pack so friction themes sit on the executive scorecard.
- **Employee understanding of the corporate story.** Change the internal plan by adding an employee baseline before leadership rollout.
