# Atom 3 — grounded verdict

## Outcome

One matrix cell accepts one fixed verdict word and one exact quote from that cell's immutable unit.
Whitespace differences are normalized, but invented or cross-unit words refuse. A quote contains at
least 25 characters unless the whole unit is shorter. A recorded judgment cannot be silently
replaced.

## Frozen real cases

Four repository-derived pages form two defect/correction pairs. The stale-door pair reproduces the
recorded contradiction between “No approach has been chosen” and the signed recommendation, then
uses the delivered corrected page that says the approach is decided. The calendar pair uses the
first delivered Step 9 toolkit with nine rows and no Months 7, 9, or 11, then the delivered corrected
twelve-month toolkit. Their source paths and SHA-256 identities are frozen into the atom request
before execution.

## Competing approaches

1. `binary-groundless`: verdicts are `weak` or `fine`; the model may return only the word. This is
   cheap and decisive but does not hand an operator the page language that caused the call.
2. `action-grounded`: verdicts are `reject`, `revise`, or `clear`; every verdict must include a
   qualifying exact unit quote. This distinguishes a blocking contradiction from a repairable
   weakness and makes the location actionable, at the cost of a stricter response contract.

Both approaches are exercised through their real candidate CLI and the mandated read-only
`codex exec` reader. The same prompt content, units, lenses, and cases are supplied to each; only
the verdict vocabulary and quote contract differ.

## Criteria fixed before execution

Rank in this order across all four cases: expected defect/correction classification, pairwise
discrimination for every applicable lens, quote groundedness, actionable quote handoff, and
actionable refusal of an invalid quote. A result that calls both members of a pair defective or
both corrected fails discrimination. Prose justification is ignored by scoring.

Lens relevance is fixed rather than forced synthetically: buyer-read, journalist,
employee-insider, and competitor-counter-position exercise the stale decision contradiction; CFO,
benchmark-vs-reference, and upstream-trace exercise the incomplete annual calendar. Together the
two recorded defects cover all seven fixed lenses.

## Stopping condition

The assembled winner records valid verdict-plus-quote judgments on all four frozen pages, each
applicable lens discriminates its real red page from its corrected twin, malformed verdicts and
quotes refuse actionably, focused tests pass, and the exact operator CLI reproduces those results.
