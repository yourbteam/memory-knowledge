# Execution Toolkit

The bridge from an approved strategy to activity on the ground: a self-contained pack any team, market or partner can run to the same standard, without the strategist in the room.

**10 of 10 assets still to be produced.** They are listed as such rather than implied to exist.

**Not approved.** The assets and the do-not-say lines are a person's to approve, and no owner has.

## A · The strategic foundation

**Platform recap.** The platform is So You Can, launched through The Invisible Thread. Vivacom shows how it keeps daily digital life working from the household outward, so people can live, work, watch, pay, study and stay close to family without thinking about the system behind it.

**Story skeleton.** Insight: People judge digital connection in ordinary moments: the home internet works, the television plays, the bill is clear, the app payment goes through, and help is there when something breaks. · Reason to Believe: Vivacom can prove one daily relationship through fixed internet, pay television, coverage-experience reach and service moments. · Benefit: People can get on with daily life because the connection stays useful, clear and supported. · Proof: The source material cites Vivacom's 2024 fixed internet subscriber share, about 60% pay-television subscriber share, OpenSignal coverage-experience strength in January 2026, and visible service touchpoints such as bills, app payments, outages and help.

**Strategy recap.** The plan is earned-led because corporate reputation needs third-party proof. Owned channels hold the full proof, shared channels carry customer, employee and community proof, and paid channels selectively drive reach and action. The story starts at home, then extends to workplaces, municipalities and public services only when proof is named, current and cleared.

## B · The plan

### Pre-Seed / Tease

**Objective.** Lock proof, claims and internal lines before any public launch activity.

**Audience.** Employees, service teams, leaders, Legal and Regulatory Affairs, media contacts and priority stakeholders.

**Messages.** Vivacom keeps The Invisible Thread working, So You Can get on with daily life. · Reach is proved carefully, not claimed broadly. · Every service moment is where The Invisible Thread is either proved or broken in public.

**Activities.** Clear claim language for reach, service and household proof. · Prepare spokesperson materials, the question and answer pack, the fact sheet and service response lines. · Brief internal teams on the approved language and the do-not-say guardrails.

### Launch

**Objective.** Introduce So You Can and The Invisible Thread through a credible household story led by earned media.

**Audience.** Existing customers, with Maya as the first believer, plus media, consumer audiences and the critical urban audience.

**Messages.** The Invisible Thread starts at home. · Vivacom is strongest when it proves the household relationship people already use. · The household relationship is Vivacom's first proof.

**Activities.** Issue the launch press release after sign-off. · Place the first household story through earned media. · Drive readers to owned proof pages with selective paid support. · Publish social assets that use approved lines and point back to proof.

### Sustain

**Objective.** Keep the idea visible through recurring household, service and reach proof.

**Audience.** Existing customers, consumer customers, employees, service teams, regulators, media and government stakeholders.

**Messages.** Vivacom keeps The Invisible Thread working from the home outward. · Coverage-experience proof supports reach, not speed, reliability or quality. · Service moments prove the idea in public.

**Activities.** Run the Household Proof Series on owned channels. · Maintain the Conversation Management loop every day. · Use The Observatory newsroom to enter relevant moments with cleared lines. · Publish short service explainers for common customer questions.

### Amplify

**Objective.** Extend the proof from homes to workplaces, municipalities and public services when evidence is cleared.

**Audience.** Business buyers, public-sector customers, municipalities, employees, parent-group stakeholders and credible third parties.

**Messages.** The same proof logic carries from the home to workplaces, municipalities and public services. · Useful relationships are stronger than broad claims about size. · The Invisible Thread works when proof is visible.

**Activities.** Publish named case stories only after partner and claim clearance. · Equip spokespeople and credible third parties with approved proof. · Use events and earned stories to show how the proof travels beyond the household. · Review performance and recalibrate each quarter.

## C · The messages & assets

### Message tracks

#### Existing customers, with Maya as the first believer

**Insight.** Existing customers judge Vivacom through ordinary household moments: internet, television, bills, app payments, outages and help.

**Benefit.** They see Vivacom as useful and warmer because the promise is shown in daily life.

**Proof.** The approved brief names existing customers as the primary first-believer audience and says Vivacom under-indexes on warmth.

**What they will say.** Vivacom keeps The Invisible Thread working, So You Can get on with daily life.

**What they will see.** A named household story, clear service explainers, proof pages and social posts that connect home internet, television, bills, app payments and help.

#### Consumer customers and the critical urban audience

**Insight.** Vivacom is most believable when it proves the relationship people already use every day.

**Benefit.** They understand the household relationship as Vivacom's first proof, not a broad claim about size.

**Proof.** The International Comparative Legal Guides report Vivacom at 39.4% of fixed internet subscribers and about 60% of pay-television subscribers in 2024.

**What they will say.** Vivacom is strongest when it proves the household relationship people already use.

**What they will see.** Recurring household stories, customer-service facts, product proof and approved paid, owned and shared lines.

#### Regulators, media, government and parent-group stakeholders

**Insight.** Coverage-experience proof can support reach, but it cannot support claims about speed, reliability or overall quality.

**Benefit.** They see a disciplined proof story that respects claim boundaries.

**Proof.** OpenSignal says Vivacom dominated coverage experience in January 2026, while A1 was fastest and Yettel led reliability and time on network.

**What they will say.** Vivacom can show reach through coverage-experience proof, not through broad network claims.

**What they will see.** Claim-cleared explainers, media lines, source notes and approved responses to competitor comparisons.

#### Employees, service teams and leaders

**Insight.** A service issue can prove or break The Invisible Thread in public.

**Benefit.** Teams know that service friction sits inside the promise and must be answered clearly.

**Proof.** The Research and Insight Pack records app reviews, Trustpilot criticism, contract penalties, outages and data-protection pressure as visible friction.

**What they will say.** Every service moment is where The Invisible Thread is either proved or broken in public.

**What they will see.** Plain-language updates that say what happened, who it affects, what Vivacom is doing and what changes for customers.

#### Business buyers, public-sector customers, municipalities, employees and parent-group stakeholders

**Insight.** The household proof logic can travel only when workplace, municipal and public-service examples are named, current and cleared.

**Benefit.** They see the idea extend beyond the home through useful relationships, not through claims about total leadership.

**Proof.** The Strategy One-Pager states that the idea starts with Maya at home, then extends the same proof logic to employees, business buyers, municipalities, regulators, media and parent-group stakeholders.

**What they will say.** The same proof logic carries from the home to workplaces, municipalities and public services.

**What they will see.** Cleared case stories from business customers, local services, schools, institutions and employees.

Spokespeople are trained to deliver them consistently before anything publishes; the tracks above are their training set.

## Influencer and creator activation

A repeatable way to activate creators, with the targets stated up front rather than negotiated each time.

**Targets, per creator.** 1 blog / long post · 3–5 social updates over ~14 days · 2–3 posts per day during a live challenge · an agreed number of pieces committed up front

**Golden rules.** be relevant & targeted · crowd-source their expertise · personalise — never cut-and-paste · make it easy — ready assets, local adaptation · be transparent and require disclosure of gifted product · be persistent with relevance

## Governance & the pre-publish gate

**Do not say.**
- Do not say coverage means speed, reliability or quality.
- Do not say Vivacom is the best network, fastest network or most reliable network in Bulgaria.
- Do not say Vivacom is total technology leader or ask people to admire its size.
- Do not say service friction is a side issue.
- Do not use the old BTK (the former state-monopoly telecom name in Bulgaria) frame as the story.

**Legal and disclosure.** Nothing publishes until the owner approves the story and proof in writing. Legal and Regulatory Affairs must clear every public claim, source note and comparison before release, especially any OpenSignal coverage-experience wording. Partner, workplace, municipal and public-service examples need written approval from the named party before use.

**The pre-publish checklist.** Self-contained · every phase has its objective, audience, messages and activities · message tracks are audience-specific with insight, benefit and proof · the collateral library is complete and on-template · fixed vs flexible is clear · measurement is wired in · governance is in place · versioned for distribution.

**Crisis tripwire.** Litigation, disclosure, deal, restructuring, life-safety and regulatory triggers escalate to a senior person before anything publishes.

**Data and AI (artificial intelligence).** Material non-public information never enters AI tools; #noAI defaults apply for restricted clients.
