## Executive summary (2–3 lines)

Core narrative: Vivacom should not ask people to admire its size. It should show how one useful relationship keeps the home connected, entertainment working, reach visible through OpenSignal coverage-experience proof, and help easier to manage.

Key message 2: Fixed internet and television make the home the first proof point.

Key message 5: The same proof logic extends to business, public-sector and employee audiences.

## Proof points / supporting evidence

The Approved Brief names existing customers as the primary first-believer audience and says Vivacom under-indexes on warmth. ICLG reports Vivacom at 39.4% of fixed internet subscribers and about 60% of pay-TV subscribers in 2024. OpenSignal says Vivacom dominated coverage experience in January 2026, while A1 was fastest and Yettel led reliability and time on network. The Research & Insight Pack records app reviews, Trustpilot criticism, contract penalties, outages and data-protection pressure as visible friction.

## Channel strategy (PESO)

PESO means paid, earned, shared and owned media. The approach should lead with earned media because corporate reputation needs third-party proof, then use owned service channels, shared customer proof and selective paid support to repeat the same story.

**Why this leads.** Earned media leads because the gap is trust, not awareness. Paid, shared and owned channels can repeat and deepen the story, but they cannot by themselves make a proof-led corporate role credible.

## Campaign timeline & phases

The rollout dates are not established. The phasing should move from proof lock, to internal alignment, to earned-led launch, to service-channel reinforcement, to quarterly measurement review.

**The phasing spine.** 1) Lock the proof and claim boundaries. 2) Align leaders and internal teams. 3) Launch through earned proof. 4) Reinforce through owned service channels. 5) Review the tracker and friction signals.

## Success metrics & KPIs

Primary measures are reputation, affinity and preference in the Kantar brand tracker. Secondary measures are Net Promoter Score, message recall tied to named proof, quality of earned attention, customer-friction themes, and employee belief. Targets are set once the latest tracker wave and launch baseline are confirmed.

# The options it was chosen from

Weak strategy is the first plausible approach written up confidently. These are the alternatives and how each scored.

## Option 1 — Make daily digital life work from the household outward

**Shape.** Reposition

**Direction.** GET Maya and the wider audiences Vivacom serves WHO see separate services and service friction TO see one useful digital relationship BY showing fixed internet, television, reach through OpenSignal coverage-experience proof, and help working together in daily life.

**Positioning.** Vivacom makes Bulgaria's daily digital life work, from the home to the institution, through proof people can see.

| Fit | It resolves the insight's tension because it starts with the household relationship and still serves the wider national role objective. |
| Distinctiveness | It is hard for A1 or Yettel to copy because it joins fixed internet, television, reach through OpenSignal coverage-experience proof, and service touchpoints in one paid relationship. |
| Feasibility | Vivacom can do it because the evidence already supports fixed internet strength, pay-TV strength, coverage-experience reach, and visible customer-service touchpoints. |
| Durability | It can last through a year and a bad news cycle if service proof remains visible and claims stay narrow. |

## Option 2 — Win warmth through service proof before reputation claims

**Shape.** Close the trust gap **Direction.** GET Maya WHO judges Vivacom through bills, app payments, outages, support and contract clarity TO believe Vivacom is becoming easier to live with BY making service fixes the first proof in the story. **Positioning.** Vivacom earns warmth by making the customer relationship clearer and easier to manage. **Rejected —** It is too narrow because it turns the corporate strategy into a service-recovery story.

## Option 3 — Turn national scale into local proof

**Shape.** Own the tension **Direction.** GET customers, employees, business buyers, municipalities, regulators and media WHO see Vivacom as large and active TO see a useful national role BY showing named Bulgarian outcomes in homes, workplaces, schools and local services. **Positioning.** Vivacom turns national scale into useful local outcomes. **Rejected —** It is too easy to pull back into scale, infrastructure and market-power scrutiny.

# UP's recommendation

**Recommended.** Make daily digital life work from the household outward

It beats the others because it has the best fit with the household insight, the strongest distinctiveness against A1 and Yettel, the clearest available proof, and the most durable route through issues pressure. It also keeps the national role without turning scale into the message.

**Coverage.** Vivacom serves consumer, business, enterprise and public-sector customers. The idea starts with Maya at home, then uses the same proof logic for employees, business buyers, municipalities, regulators, media and parent-group stakeholders: show the useful relationship, prove the service, clear the claim.

**Why not the others.** Win warmth through service proof before reputation claims — It is too narrow because it turns the corporate strategy into a service-recovery story. Turn national scale into local proof — It is too easy to pull back into scale, infrastructure and market-power scrutiny.
