# Experiment machinery: three verified atoms

1. **Independent judging.** Version 5 presents selected raw output with a frozen independent reference. Complete evidence-bound observations produce official scores in code. Candidate counters cannot manufacture a better score, and quote length alone earns no actionability credit. The observation approach beat accepting numeric evaluator scores.
2. **Qualified winners.** Essential minimums apply to every original case before aggregation. Exact top ties report no demonstrated advantage. The per-case approach beat aggregate-only gates because a good case no longer conceals a bad one. No recommendation prevents composition.
3. **Early evaluator checks.** Positive and negative calibration cases exercise the actual evaluator before candidates launch. This beat response-shape checks alone: both incompatible and always-pass judges were refused before execution. The incompatible judge launched zero candidates with calibration, versus two without it.

All three atoms passed controlled comparisons, final assembly checks, and the promoted operator path on recorded success and failure cases. The final regression run passed **147 tests**. A complete four-case calibrated pipeline passed; an always-pass judge stopped all four cases before candidate execution or composition. Independent final review found no blocking defects.

New quality experiments should provide assessment and calibration as specified in the skill. Historical requests retain their recorded behavior. Calibration adds one evaluator invocation per captured case; no production latency percentage or universal semantic accuracy is claimed.
