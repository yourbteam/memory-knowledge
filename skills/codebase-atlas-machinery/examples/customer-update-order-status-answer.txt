The handler sets the order status to command.StatusId and Updated to DateTime.UtcNow, then awaits dbContext.SaveChangesAsync(cancellationToken) before initializing eventLink or constructing the response. eventLink starts as null and is populated only when command.StatusId is 6 and both TourTimeSlotsId and TimeSlotDate have values; its URL is built from _options.PublicAppBaseUrl.TrimEnd('/') plus /customer/images/{slot id}/{date formatted yyyy-MM-dd}. The response constructor receives, in order, eventLink, CustomerCheckoutSupport.ResolveHomepageLink(order.TourTimeSlot!.Tour!, _options), and the raw order.TourTimeSlot.Tour!.HomepageUrl.
